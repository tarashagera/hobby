My Paper Plane Project

A small sheet of paper can become something that flies. Let us make a classic dart paper plane!

Paper-folding inspiration for my first build.

Build a Classic Dart

This project uses one sheet of paper, with no glue or scissors needed.

Materials Needed

  • One rectangular sheet of paper, such as A4 paper
  • A flat surface for folding
  • Colour pencils for decorating (optional)

How to Fold the Plane

  1. Place the paper on a table with a short edge at the top.
  2. Fold it in half lengthwise. Press the fold, then open the paper again.
  3. Fold both top corners inward so they meet at the centre crease.
  4. Fold the two upper sloping edges inward again so they meet at the centre crease. The nose should now be narrow.
  5. Fold the plane in half along the centre crease, keeping the previous folds on the outside.
  6. Fold one side down to make a wing, lining its upper sloping edge up with the bottom of the body. Turn the plane over and repeat.
  7. Lift the wings slightly so they are level with each other. Hold the body underneath and gently launch the plane.
Tips for Your First Flight
  • Make neat folds and press each crease firmly.
  • Keep both wings the same size.
  • Try a gentle, level throw in an open space.
  • Change one thing at a time and see how the next flight changes.
A Small Safety Reminder

Fly your plane away from faces, roads and fragile objects.
